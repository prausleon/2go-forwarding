## Architecture principles

1. Service pages are first-class landing pages, not generic cards.
2. Every service page should answer: what it is, who it is for, cargo types, coverage, process, capabilities, proof points, FAQs and CTA.
3. Industry pages should map business problems to services rather than repeat service descriptions.
4. Network pages should expose geographic entities and route context in crawlable HTML.
5. FAQ content should target natural-language customer questions and support answer-engine retrieval.
6. Avoid making broader 2GO corporate capabilities appear to be Forwarding products unless the source/current business owner confirms the relationship.
7. Keep claims such as speed, lead times, capacity and coverage tied to source-backed/current facts.

## Homepage architecture

Header → Hero → Network proof metrics → Core services → Why 2GO → Specialized solutions → Industries → How logistics works → Network → FAQs → CTA → Footer.

## Conversion architecture

Primary CTA: Request a Quote
Secondary CTA: Explore Services
Contextual CTA: Talk to a Logistics Specialist

The quote form should capture origin, destination, cargo type, approximate volume/weight, mode preference, timing and contact details.
