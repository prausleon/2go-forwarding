Cargo Safety

Dangerous Goods

Some cargo requires special handling, documentation and transport controls. Use this page as the information architecture for the final 2GO Forwarding dangerous-goods guidance.

Important

Dangerous-goods acceptance depends on the applicable transport mode, cargo classification, packaging and current 2GO requirements. The source material identifies dangerous/prohibited cargo categories, but the production website should use the latest approved 2GO dangerous-goods rules before publication.

Categories identified in the source material

Explosives

Cargo capable of explosive reaction or containing explosive substances.

Gases

Compressed, liquefied or otherwise regulated gases.

Flammable liquids

Liquids presenting a flammability hazard during transport.

Poisons

Toxic or poisonous substances requiring regulated handling.

Batteries

Battery shipments may require specific acceptance, packaging and documentation controls.

Magnetic substances

Magnetic materials identified among restricted cargo categories in the source deck.

Oxidizing materials

Materials capable of supporting or intensifying combustion.

Certain weapons

Weapons and related items may be prohibited or subject to transport restrictions.

Need to ship a regulated item?

Do not assume that an item is accepted because it is not listed here. Contact 2GO Forwarding for the current requirements before tendering cargo.
