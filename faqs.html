FAQs

Questions about moving cargo?

Answer-first content designed for customers and future search/AI discovery.

What transportation modes does 2GO Forwarding offer?

The supplied Forwarding materials describe sea, air and land cargo transportation, with integrated logistics solutions across the Philippines.

What is LCL?

LCL, or Less-than-Container Load, is consolidated cargo. The source materials identify Domestic LCL, Domestic LCL Shipside, Domestic LCL Plus and LCL Cold Chain.

What is Domestic LCL Shipside for?

It is positioned for urgent shipments and perishables, with shorter loading cut-off, priority loading and priority cargo releasing.

Does 2GO Forwarding support temperature-controlled cargo?

Yes. The supplied materials describe LCL Cold Chain and pharmaceutical logistics, including temperature-controlled storage and delivery.

Can 2GO transport motorcycles?

Yes. The materials describe motorcycle delivery and a reusable, collapsible steel crating solution designed for protection.

Does 2GO Forwarding provide air freight?

Yes. Air freight is positioned as a time-definite option with guaranteed booking and uplift efficiency through domestic airline relationships.

Does 2GO Forwarding support retail and mall logistics?

Yes. The materials describe mall logistics covering store replenishment, inter-mall transfers, reverse logistics, returns, RTV management and peak-season capacity.

How do I request a logistics solution?

Use the Request a Quote journey and provide origin, destination, cargo type, approximate volume or weight, timing and special handling requirements.
